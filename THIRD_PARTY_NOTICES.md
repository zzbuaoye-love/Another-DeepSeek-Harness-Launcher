# Third-party assets

- Sidebar icons: [Lucide](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide), ISC License. The included SVGs use a 2.5 stroke width. The license text is in `Assets/Icons/LICENSE`.
- DeepSeek Harness icon: [official DeepSeek Harness Windows SVG](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/apps/desktop/resources/icon-windows.svg), MIT License. The license text is in `Assets/DeepSeekHarness-LICENSE`. It appears when a local DSH package is detected or the launcher starts DSH. AnotherDSHL is an independent launcher and is not affiliated with DeepSeek.
- Embedded PackForge engine: `@dshpackforge/core` and `@dshpackforge/host-node` 0.1.1 from [DSH-PackForge](https://github.com/DSH-PackForge/dsh-packforge-app/tree/3d64d2bf96a5d792f05bb34396713c43b9eba09c), declared MIT in their package manifests. Unmodified upstream sources are in `packforge/vendor`; provenance and license are in `Resources/PackForge/LICENSE.txt`. The bundle includes [fflate](https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate) 0.8.2 under MIT, with its license in `Resources/PackForge/FFLATE-LICENSE.txt`.
